queued: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Summary

queued is a calendar app that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer never receives your data.

What the app accesses

  • Calendars on your iPhone. With your permission, queued reads and changes events in the calendars set up on the device, such as iCloud.
  • Google Calendar. If you sign in to a Google account, queued reads your calendars and events and makes the changes you ask for: creating, editing and deleting events, inviting guests and adding Google Meet links.
  • Your Google account's email address, to show which account is connected.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show your schedule in the app and its widgets and to carry out the edits you make. It is not used for any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Events and settings are stored on your iPhone, in storage shared only between the app and its own widgets. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on the device. Diagnostic logs stay on the phone and do not contain tokens, event titles, attendee addresses or notes.

Who the data is shared with

No one. The app communicates only with Apple's calendar services on the device and with Google's sign-in and Calendar services, directly from your phone. Data is not sold, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

queued's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Removing an account in the app deletes that account's stored events and sign-in tokens from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Notifications

queued does not send notifications or reminders of its own.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub page.
