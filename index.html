queued

queued is a personal calendar app for iPhone. It shows the day's events from your iCloud and Google calendars as a single timeline, in the app and in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and lets you add and edit events.

The app is built for its developer's own use and is not distributed through the App Store.

queued asks for access to Google Calendar so it can read and change the events in the Google accounts you sign in to. It has no server: your calendar data goes only between your phone and Google.

Privacy policy

queued is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google or Apple.
